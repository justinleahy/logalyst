Logalyst

Log the health data your Apple Watch can't measure, straight into Apple Health, from your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Vitals and body

Blood pressure, blood glucose, temperature, weight, body fat and more, prefilled with your last reading.

Food and water

Track water, calories and nutrients against daily goals. Scan barcodes or nutrition labels, save foods, log by servings, weight or measured volume, and put together meals and recipes. See when nutrition is missing or a total is partial, and fix anything you've logged.

Symptoms and events

Record symptoms with severity, inhaler use, toothbrushing and more, so everything lives in one place in Health.

Everywhere you are

Widgets, Lock Screen and Control Center buttons, Watch complications, Siri and Shortcuts, and reminders to log.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your health data stays in Apple Health on your devices. Read the policy.
